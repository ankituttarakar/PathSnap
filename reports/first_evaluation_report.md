# PathSnap — First Evaluation Report

*Scope: the two first-evaluation marking categories — (1) Data Preprocessing &
Feature Engineering, (2) Model Selection & Training. All numbers below are
produced by committed, reproducible code over the **train** (80) and
**student_test** (10) partitions. The evaluator partition was never extracted,
read, or used. Raw data is unchanged (md5 of `data/raw` identical before/after).*

Generated by: `scripts/build_dataset.py` and `scripts/run_training.py`.
Seed: 42. Group-aware CV: `GroupKFold(trip_id)`, 5 folds.

---

## 1. Completed implementation

| Stage | Module | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Projection/geometry | `pathsnap/geo.py` | Per-trip local **UTM** projection (metres), point→segment projection, bearings, geodesic distance. |
| Preprocessing | `pathsnap/preprocess.py` | Builds projected observations + directed arc geometry; **Δt**, consecutive speed, **outlier flagging (not deletion)**, **gap segmentation** (Δt>30 s), original `arc_id` preserved. |
| Label derivation | `pathsnap/labels.py` | **Monotonic projection of GPS onto the ordered route polyline** (direction-preserving, handles repeated arcs & broken links; off-route points flagged `confident=False`). Not blind nearest-road. |
| Candidate generation | `pathsnap/candidates.py` | **KD-tree** over arc midpoints → refine by true segment distance → ≤8 candidates within 60 m. Measures **recall / miss-rate**. Route-free (inference-valid). |
| Features | `pathsnap/features.py` | 12 leakage-safe features per (obs, candidate). **No `.route`-derived feature.** |
| Group CV | `pathsnap/cv.py` | `GroupKFold(trip_id)` with an assertion that no trip spans folds. |
| Models | `pathsnap/models.py` | 5 models across families; Pipelines with scaling where needed. |
| Evaluation | `pathsnap/evaluation.py` | Candidate macro-F1/precision/recall/log-loss, **observation Top-1**, **route continuity**. |

Runnable end-to-end: `python scripts/build_dataset.py` then
`python scripts/run_training.py`.

## 2. Data preprocessing & feature engineering (Category 1)

**Projection.** Each trip is projected to its local UTM zone (chosen from the
trip centroid) so distances/headings are metric. Justified by the global spread
of trips (a single CRS would distort distance).

**Segmentation & gaps.** A new trajectory segment starts when Δt > 30 s; motion
features are not carried across a break. Train has long gaps (max ~42.5 h).

**Outliers.** Consecutive speed > 55 m/s flags a point as suspicious; flagged
points are **retained** (not deleted) and only excluded from speed smoothing.
Train flags are concentrated in a few trips (consistent with Phase-1 EDA).

**Label derivation (observation → arc).** The `.route` gives an *ordered* arc
sequence but no row alignment. We project each GPS point onto the route polyline
with a **monotonic forward pointer** (window 25 arcs, back-tolerance 1), which
preserves direction, disambiguates repeated arcs, and tolerates broken links
**without inventing connector arcs**. Points whose perpendicular distance exceeds
50 m are flagged `confident=False` and excluded from supervised labels.

Measured (mean over trips):

| partition | label coverage (confident) | median proj. distance | candidate recall | candidate miss-rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | **96.3%** | ~4–6 m | **96.9%** | **3.1%** |
| student_test | **98.0%** | ~4–5 m | **97.7%** | **2.3%** |

Per-trip detail: `outputs/eda/dataset_diagnostics_{train,student_test}.csv`.

**Features (12, all inference-available, no route leakage):** `dist_m`, `t`
(position along arc), `rank`, `n_cand`, `dist_margin_m`, `arc_len`,
`heading_diff`, `speed_ms`, `dt_s`, `is_outlier`, `seg_start`, `cum_gps_frac`.
The label column is derived from the route for **training only** and is never a
feature (verified programmatically — no `route`/`label`/`along` feature exists).

## 3. Model selection & training (Category 2)

Framing: **per-candidate probabilistic classification** — predict P(candidate is
the true arc); per observation the argmax candidate is the Top-1 prediction.
Five models from distinct families, group-aware 5-fold CV on the 80 train trips;
`student_test` used for **development comparison only** (never fitted).

### 3.1 Actual results (real, reproducible)

Dataset: train = **1,104,848** confident candidate rows (178,685 positives);
student_test = **113,805** rows (18,057 positives).

| model | family | CV macro-F1 | CV Top-1 | student Top-1 | student macro-F1 | student log-loss | route continuity | CV fit time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **random_forest** | Ensemble (bagging) | **0.779** | 0.557 | **0.558** | 0.772 | 0.334 | 0.183 | 146 s |
| extra_trees | Ensemble (rand. trees) | 0.776 | 0.553 | 0.541 | 0.770 | 0.360 | 0.122 | 67 s |
| logistic_regression | Linear | 0.748 | 0.553 | 0.538 | 0.744 | 0.390 | 0.109 | 5 s |

### 3.2 Honest interpretation

- **Naive baseline:** picking the geometrically nearest candidate gives Top-1
  **0.553 (train) / 0.538 (student)**. So at the observation level the trained
  classifiers only modestly beat "nearest arc" — **Random Forest is best at
  0.558 (+2.0 pts over naive on student)**. This is expected: single-observation
  geometry dominates, and the large uplift should come from **sequential
  decoding (HMM/Viterbi)**, which is Phase 8 and **not yet implemented**.
- **Macro-F1 ≈ 0.74–0.77** shows the candidate classifier is genuinely learning
  the binary "is this the right arc?" task (well above trivial).
- **CV ≈ student Top-1** (0.557 vs 0.558 for RF) → no meaningful
  overfitting/optimism gap; group-aware CV generalises.
- **Log-loss:** HistGradientBoosting is best-calibrated (0.240); Gaussian NB is
  poorly calibrated (0.808) despite comparable F1.
- **Route continuity is low (0.11–0.18)** for every model — correct and expected
  for a *classifier-only* stage that treats observations independently; enforcing
  network connectivity is exactly the job of the HMM (Phase 8).
- **Model selection (preliminary):** by CV macro-F1 and student Top-1, **Random
  Forest** is the current front-runner; HistGradientBoosting is the calibration
  front-runner and the natural candidate for Phase-7 Bayesian HPO. Final
  selection is deferred until after HPO + HMM, per the guidelines.

## 4. Leakage & integrity controls (verified)

- `GroupKFold(trip_id)` with an in-code assertion that no trip spans folds.
- `assert_no_group_overlap(train, student_test)` before training.
- Features contain **no** route/label-derived column (checked in code).
- `student_test` never fitted; evaluator partition never extracted/read.
- Raw data md5 unchanged across the whole pipeline.

## 5. Remaining limitations / gaps

- **Observation-level Top-1 (~0.55) is modest** and close to the nearest-arc
  baseline; the sequential model (HMM/Viterbi, Phase 8) is required for the
  expected accuracy and route-continuity gains — **not yet implemented**.
- **Candidate miss-rate 3.1%** (train) caps achievable Top-1 at ~0.97; tuning the
  candidate radius/k is future work.
- **No Bayesian HPO yet** (Phase 7): models use justified initial configs only.
- Tree models use capped depth/estimators for tractability on ~1.1M rows; HPO
  may change this.
- Confusion matrices and per-model deep-dives are summarised, not yet exhaustive.
